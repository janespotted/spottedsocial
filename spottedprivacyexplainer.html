How location privacy works on Spotted

Three sharing levels, and one rule that explains all of it.
The one rule
Your privacy setting controls who can see you — and only you. It says nothing about who you can see. Everyone has their own setting.

The three levels — who's allowed to see you

Close Friends
Only people on your Close Friends list. The tightest circle — you pick exactly who's in it.
All Friends
Anyone you're friends with. Your full accepted-friends list.
Mutual
Friends — plus friends of your friends. The widest circle: anyone who shares at least one friend with you.

Two things that only go one way

🤝 Close Friends is one-directional
You add Sam→Sam is your close friend
You✕…but you're not automatically Sam's
Your Close Friends list is yours alone. Adding someone doesn't add you to theirs — they choose their own list, separately.
👁️ Visibility is one-directional too
You see Sam✕doesn't mean Sam sees you
Whether you see Sam depends on Sam's setting. Whether Sam sees you depends on your setting. Two separate checks — they can disagree.

How the app decides "can A see B?" — in order

1
Blocked? If either has blocked the other → no. Stop.
2
Hidden? If B specifically hid their location from A → no. Stop.
3
Check B's sharing level: Close Friends → is A on B's close list? · All Friends → are they friends? · Mutual → friends, or friends-of-friends?
4
Qualifies → A sees B. Otherwise → hidden. This runs separately for each direction.
Spotted — location privacy model. Your dial controls who sees you; their dial controls who sees them.
